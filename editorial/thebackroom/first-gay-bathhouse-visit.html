Your first visit to a gay bathhouse

What to check before you go, what to bring, and how to handle consent and privacy on a first visit.

Check admission before leaving home

Read the venue’s rules on age, eligibility, government ID, membership, bag checks, and re-entry. Look for current locker and room rates. If the website leaves out access details that matter to you, call the venue before making the trip.

Bring only what you need

Start with the ID and payment method the venue accepts. Check whether towels or lockers are included and whether the venue restricts phones, bags, or outside items. Ask staff where to store your things and which areas are quiet, social, or private.

Take the first visit at your own pace

Give yourself time to learn the layout. A glance, a conversation, or entering a shared space is separate from agreeing to touch. Ask first and pause if either person changes their mind. Respect photography rules and keep other visitors’ identities private. It’s fine to spend the visit getting comfortable or to leave early.
