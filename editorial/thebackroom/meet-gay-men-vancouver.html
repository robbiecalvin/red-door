The Backroom

Where to meet gay men in Vancouver beyond the bars

Try recurring queer social groups, sports, and culture in Vancouver, with practical tips for showing up solo.

Choose an activity you’d enjoy anyway

A shared activity can take pressure off the first conversation. Vancouver’s queer groups welcome people across the community. Go to participate and make friends, and let any other connection develop on its own.

  • Vancouver Gaymers is a starting point for board games or video games. Ask the host if they can point you to a table with an open seat or a beginner-friendly game.
  • Vancouver Frontrunners has walking and running. Check the latest gathering details and choose a pace you can comfortably talk at.
  • Van Queer Climbers provides registration links for different gyms. Confirm rental equipment, entry costs, and how to find the group when you arrive.

Try a film or a peer group

Browse Out On Screen for queer film programming. A screening with a discussion gives you an easy opening question afterward. For peer-led spaces, look at QMUNITY’s current programs and choose one whose purpose and participation criteria fit.

Make it easy to go back

Pick one recurring event and try it twice. On the first visit, introduce yourself to the host and ask how the gathering works. If you click with someone, offer to swap details or suggest seeing each other at the next event. Give them room to decide.
