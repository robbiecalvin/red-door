Vancouver

Gay Vancouver guide

Find LGBTQ+ nightlife, recurring social groups, and community resources in Vancouver.

Pick a starting point based on the kind of evening you want. For a first solo visit, a scheduled show or a structured activity can make it easier to arrive on your own.

Official listings checked November 23, 2026. Check the organizer’s current listing before travelling for schedules, accessibility, cover, and admission.

Nightlife and adult venues

  • The Junction · Davie Village. Choose a scheduled drag show, trivia, or live music from the venue calendar.
  • The Fountainhead Pub · Davie Village. A pub option if you want to start the evening with a conversation. See the pub’s contact page for hours.
  • F212 Steam · West End. Adult sauna and gym. Check the venue’s admission and rates before visiting.
  • Steamworks Vancouver · Downtown. Adult bathhouse, sauna, and gym. Read the club rules and rates first.

New to adult venues? Read what to expect on your first bathhouse visit.

Recurring groups

  • Vancouver Gaymers · Metro Vancouver. Board games, video games, and other social events. Check each event’s venue and age rules.
  • Vancouver Frontrunners · Vancouver. LGBTQ2+ running and walking. Check meetup details and pick a pace that suits you.
  • Van Queer Climbers · Several Metro Vancouver gyms. Check the organizer’s current registration links and the host gym’s entry requirements.

See where to meet gay men in Vancouver beyond the bars for ideas on choosing an event and showing up solo.

Community and events

  • QMUNITY · Vancouver. Find peer groups, counselling, and resources for queer, trans, and Two-Spirit people.
  • Vancouver Queer Film Festival · Multiple venues. Out On Screen posts programming and ticket details on its festival website.
  • What’s On Queer BC · Lower Mainland and beyond. Use the current calendar to find one-off events and confirm details with the host.
