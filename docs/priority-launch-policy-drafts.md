# Badehaus X launch policy package

The owner approved the policy drafts on November 19, 2026. The owner excluded the operator name, jurisdiction, and support and privacy contact fields from this launch scope. The policy pages have not been published.

## Terms of Service

Badehaus X is for adults aged 18 and older. Use accurate age and account information. Keep your credentials private and tell support if someone else accesses your account. You are responsible for what you submit and for getting permission before posting another person's image, private information or words.

You retain ownership of your submissions. You give Badehaus X permission to host, display and moderate the material as needed to provide the features you use. Public articles may be read by visitors. Editorial submissions are reviewed before publication unless published directly by the operator. You can ask support to remove a contribution. Discuss any third-party rights or publication permissions with the editor before submitting.

Badehaus X may restrict access or remove material for safety, legal or policy reasons. You can ask for a review through the approved support channel.

## Privacy Policy

We handle account details, optional profile fields, information used to run discovery and location features, messages and uploads, reports, membership and payment records, and your communication preferences. The service uses this information to operate your account, help you connect, process payments, investigate reports and improve the product. Payment processing is handled by Stripe. Confirm the full list of processors, data locations, retention schedules and deletion exceptions before publication.

Choose what you disclose in your profile. Precise location shared in a conversation should be treated as sensitive. Keep other people's information private. E-transfer contacts are used for payout administration. Any approved payout sheet must be private and limited to authorized operators.

You can request access, correction or deletion where available. The operator should document how it verifies and responds to such requests. Confirm consent and retention rules for analytics, SMS, marketing email and age information.

## Community and moderation

Treat other people with respect. Harassment, threats, impersonation, doxxing, spam, discrimination, exploitation, non-consensual content, and sexual content involving or depicting minors are prohibited. Do not share another person's media or private messages without permission. Use in-app reporting and blocking controls when appropriate. Moderators may review reported material, remove it, restrict accounts, and escalate serious concerns.

Badehaus X is sex-worker inclusive. Users may not post rates or direct links to pages that contain rates. Before publication, the operator must confirm where this rule applies and how reports and appeals will be reviewed.

Before enabling new member-written content features, publish the applicable eligibility, review, reporting and appeal rules and confirm provider eligibility. Record moderation decisions and restrict report access to authorized reviewers.

## Membership, referral payments and adjustments

Checkout shows the purchase price, term and any renewal terms. Confirm cancellation instructions and statutory refund rights with the operator before publication. Complimentary access, referral offers and paid subscriptions are subject to the terms presented when activated.

Priority referral commissions are CAD $5 for each qualifying payment confirmed by Stripe. Self-referrals, unqualified purchases and duplicate credits are excluded. Payout review uses 11 p.m. Vancouver time on the third Friday. Eligible unpaid amounts carry forward. The operator records a manual e-transfer after it is sent. A confirmed loss on a paid commission can offset future amounts. Any unapplied deduction carries forward without an automatic debit.

## Newsletter and invitations

Newsletter registration is optional. Each marketing newsletter includes an unsubscribe link. Verify recipient consent and the sending configuration before sending. Verify SMS consent for each invitation recipient and test the invitation link and opt-out instructions using an authorized test number before distribution.
