Vancouver After Dark

A local’s starting point for finding queer Vancouver beyond one strip

By Badehaus X

Davie Village is an obvious starting point for a night out in Vancouver. Give yourself time to wander, meet a friend, or make a second stop if the first one doesn’t suit your mood. A good Vancouver night might also take you to a dance event, a queer film screening, or a community gathering somewhere across town.

Try building the evening around what you actually want to do. If you want conversation, look for a quieter event or meet for a drink before a show. If you’re in the mood to dance, check queer promoter listings for pop-up events and confirm the venue, hours, ticket requirements, and accessibility directly. Check your trip home before you head out, especially for an event away from your usual route.

Badehaus X can help you see who’s nearby while you make a plan. Open Explore, share as much or as little about your evening as you like, and suggest a public meeting spot when you both feel ready. If you’re out with friends, keep an easy way to reconnect with them. A night in a new neighbourhood is more fun when everyone can go at their own pace.
