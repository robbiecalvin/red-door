Badehaus X · Policies

Community Guidelines

Effective November 19, 2026.

Consent and respect

Badehaus X is for adults. Respect personal boundaries and give people room to decline or change their minds. Harassment, discrimination, threats, fraud, impersonation, doxxing and exploitation are prohibited. Do not share another person’s private information, conversations or intimate media without permission. Sexual content involving minors and non-consensual sexual content are prohibited.

Sex-worker inclusion

Badehaus X welcomes sex workers. Users may not post rates or direct links to pages containing rates.

Report or block

Use the in-app reporting and blocking controls if you encounter unsafe or prohibited conduct. You can block someone even when you choose to leave the interaction without filing a report.
