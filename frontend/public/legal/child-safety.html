Badehaus X · Policies

Child Safety Standards

Badehaus X is for adults aged 18 and older. We prohibit child sexual abuse and exploitation in all forms, including grooming, solicitation, sextortion, trafficking, and creating, asking for, sharing, or linking to child sexual abuse material. These standards apply to profiles, messages, groups, images, videos, and any other content or conduct on the service.

How to report

Use the report action on a profile, message, photo, or video. Choose Underage concern if you believe a member is under 18. Choose Suspected child sexual abuse or exploitation for suspected exploitation. Submit the report from the content it concerns. Avoid making or forwarding copies of the material.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. In Canada, suspected online child sexual exploitation can be reported directly to Cybertip.ca.

How we respond

Child-safety reports go to a restricted moderation queue. We may restrict content or an account, preserve necessary evidence securely, and take enforcement action up to permanent removal. We report confirmed child sexual abuse material to the appropriate authority in accordance with applicable law.
