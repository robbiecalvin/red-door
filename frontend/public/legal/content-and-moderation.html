Content and Moderation Policy

Effective November 19, 2026.

Posting and submissions

Share only material you have permission to use. Priority members may submit articles to The Backroom. Member submissions stay private during editorial review. Approved articles can be read publicly.

Reports and decisions

Authorized moderators review reports. Depending on the circumstances, Badehaus X may remove material, restrict account features or suspend access. Affected users may request a review. Keep personal details and sensitive media out of public discussions about a report.
