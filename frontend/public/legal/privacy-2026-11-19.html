Access may also be required by law.

Retention and choices

We keep information for the time needed to provide the relevant feature or meet safety, security, billing, and legal obligations. You can edit your profile, change location and notification settings, unsubscribe, and use block or report controls. Privacy rights may include access or correction under applicable law. Information shared with other members may remain in their possession.
