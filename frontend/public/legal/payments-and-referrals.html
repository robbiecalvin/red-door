Badehaus X · Policies

Subscriptions, Refunds and Priority Referrals

Effective November 19, 2026.

Subscriptions and cancellation

Checkout shows the price, billing period and renewal terms before you authorize payment. You can manage your subscription in Settings under Membership. Canceling stops future renewal according to the applicable billing terms. Statutory consumer and refund rights apply.

Referral offer

Eligible customers using a Priority referral code receive three months of Badehaus X Plus for CAD $14.99, followed by CAD $9.99 per month until canceled. A qualifying Stripe-confirmed payment earns the referring Priority member CAD $5. Self-referrals and duplicate credits are excluded. Pending, failed and unverified payments do not qualify.

Manual payout review

The payout cutoff is 11 p.m. Vancouver time on the third Friday of each month. Eligible unpaid amounts carry forward. Completed e-transfers are recorded after the transfer is sent. Confirmed chargeback losses can reduce future payouts. Any unapplied adjustment carries forward without an automatic debit.
