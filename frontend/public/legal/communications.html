Badehaus X · Policies

Newsletter and Invitations

Effective November 19, 2026.

Newsletter preferences

Newsletter subscription is optional. Change your preference in Settings or follow the unsubscribe link in a newsletter. Transactional messages about activity such as account verification and billing are handled separately.

Priority invitations

Priority is invitation-only. Invitation recipients can choose whether to redeem an invitation. A referral code shared by an existing Priority member is a separate Plus offer.
