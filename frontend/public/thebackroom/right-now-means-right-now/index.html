Right Now Means Right Now

Make a spontaneous plan with someone who’s actually free

By Badehaus X

Sometimes you have an hour free and want company. It can feel awkward to say that outright, especially when “maybe later” is the default. A simple message like “I’m free until nine if you want to grab a drink” gives the other person something concrete to respond to.

On Badehaus X, Right Now lets other people know you’re available. Set the status when it reflects what you want, and change it when your plans change. If you see someone with Right Now on, open with a question about what they’re up for. Availability is a starting point for a conversation. Give each other time to decide what feels good.

For a plan on short notice, keep the arrangements simple. Offer a time and a public spot, agree on what you’ll do if either of you is delayed, and make sure you can get there and back independently. If it falls through, a quick message is plenty. When it works, you get to spend the hour together instead of using it to plan next week.
