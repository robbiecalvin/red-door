Hosting 101

A private gathering works best when the expectations are clear

By Badehaus X

Before inviting anyone, decide what kind of evening you want to host. A small dinner, a few drinks, and an intimate gathering call for different invitations. Say who is invited, roughly when the evening will end, and whether people can bring a guest. Give everyone a chance to decide privately whether they want to come.

Before sharing an address, confirm who is coming and check that everyone is comfortable with the plan. Ask before adding someone to a group conversation or sharing their details. Talk through photos, any house rules, and how people can leave whenever they want. Everyone can change their mind about attending or participating at any point.

If you’re coordinating through Badehaus X, use a group chat after people agree to join it. Keep changes about timing and the guest list in one place. Share the location only with the people who need it. On the night, make introductions, help guests settle in, and check in if someone seems uncomfortable. Clear plans give everyone more room to enjoy themselves.
