Are Gay Dating Apps Killing the Art of Actually Meeting?

How to turn a good conversation into a plan

By Badehaus X

Ten open conversations can feel busy without giving you anything to look forward to. If one person keeps making you smile, give that chat a little more attention. Ask a question you genuinely want answered. See how it feels to talk for a few minutes without checking the grid again.

Once the interest is mutual, make a small invitation. “Want to grab coffee on Commercial Drive this weekend?” is easier to respond to than “We should hang sometime.” Give the other person an easy way to suggest a different time or decline. If you’re meeting for the first time, choose somewhere public where either of you can leave independently.

Badehaus X gives you a place to start with Explore and keep talking in your Inbox. The profile can help you find an opening. The conversation is where you get a sense of each other. If the timing feels right, offer a place and time. A walk or a drink can be plenty for a first meeting.
