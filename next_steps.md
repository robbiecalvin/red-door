## Campaign · The Red Door Is Open

**Positioning.** An 18+ Vancouver-first launch for gay men who want an easy way to explore nearby, introduce themselves and start a conversation on their own terms. The creative starts with a red-lit doorway, then shows one ordinary action per post. The homepage is the primary destination. Use Backroom articles as evergreen entry points once the public page is verified.

**Proposed hero copy.** “Vancouver, say hello. Badehaus X is a space for gay men 18+ to explore nearby, share what feels right, and start a conversation.” CTA **Explore Badehaus X**. Use an original red-door visual or the approved graphics. Avoid unverified claims about how many members are online, who authored messages, convenience of payment or availability of any gated feature.

### Build distribution around the city

- **Partnerships.** Begin with a proposed three to five opt-in Vancouver collaborators across queer community media, event organizers and adult venues. Give each a separate public QR or tagged link and the same truthful, age-scoped brief. Confirm brand approval and rights before using any partner logo.
- **Editorial and search.** Verify canonical links and share previews on mobile. Link public Backroom articles to the entrance and related stories. Prioritize concrete Vancouver topics from the scheduled series, such as low-key meetup spots, with evergreen assets partners can reuse.
- **Creative and moderation.** Use released assets, captions and alt text. Demo accounts and cleared imagery protect member privacy. Assign an on-call reviewer during scheduled posting windows, agree how to handle outing, threats, harassment and impersonation, and move account-specific support to a private channel.
- **Budget.** Start organic and record partner/creative costs. A proposed CAD $300 ceiling for a one-week paid experiment is contingent on owner approval and the platform explicitly allowing promotion of this service and destination. If paid channels are ineligible, continue with organic editorial and opt-in partner distribution. Keep source links free of account identifiers and avoid audience targeting based on inferred sexual orientation.

### Measure and decide weekly

Use a consistent convention such as `utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=red_door_vancouver&utm_content=w1_door`. Record link and placement per asset. Report platform reach/clicks, visits where available, and separate site totals for verified registrations, observed profile completion, first messages, first replies, mature seven-day retention, and real paid conversions. Report consented denominators and sample sizes. A conversation can be measured only when both participants enroll in activity measurement. Counts without attribution stay at site level. The approved campaign’s post-level CTR can be compared without tying identities across platforms. [Measurement contract](docs/baseline-analytics.md)

Treat the first week as a baseline. If impressions are healthy and clicks lag, revise the hook and preview. If clicks arrive but registration stalls, inspect entrance, verification delivery and OAuth before spending more. If signups arrive but replies lag, improve onboarding and partner recruitment with real consenting members. Hold the affected promotion for privacy or moderation problems, broken signup, incorrect pricing, undisclosed automation or failed unsubscribe. At the end of Week 4, review which creative and partnerships drove measurable engagement before extending to another city.

**Release sequence.** Complete the technical and trust gates, rehearse with the controlled Vancouver cohort, review evidence with the release and moderation owners, then schedule the approved calendar. Approve newsletter, SMS invitations, payout communications, paid placement and optional feature announcements on their own evidence.
